Getting started

  1. Use Chrome or Edge (Firefox and Safari cannot talk to USB serial devices).
  2. Plug the kit into the computer with a data USB-C cable (some cables only charge).
  3. Click Connect kit and choose the port named like Feather ESP32-S2 Reverse TFT (COMx).
  4. With one coil, leave Coil 2 connected unticked: everything about Coil 2 is hidden and left out of recordings. Tick it when the second coil is plugged into A2–A3.
  5. The kit's Faraday page shows USB LINK. Use the motor panel on the left to set a speed. The kit's own buttons and screen pages keep working (D1 changes page).

Experiments

  • Oscilloscope – watch the EMF from both coils. With points ticked, every measured sample is marked; the line between them is only a guide. Sample spacing below the screen shows the average and the largest gap between samples. Auto – rising edge keeps the waveform still. Use Split to compare shapes, Overlay to compare timing (phase).
  • Faraday's law – record amplitude at several speeds; the page fits ε₀ = kω. The slope k equals N·B·A (units V·s = Wb-turns).
  • Lenz's law – stop the motor, choose Single shot, click Arm, then bring a magnet up to the coil and take it away (or drop it through). The capture shows two opposite-polarity pulses. For each pulse the page shows its peak, its width at half height, and its area ∫ε dt (shaded). A faster movement gives a taller, narrower pulse, but both pulses should enclose about the same area: the flux change going in equals the flux change coming out. With Time / div on Auto, a capture covers 2 s (0.5 s before the trigger); choose a shorter setting for quick movements such as a dropped magnet.

Good to know

  • Each coil input is limited to ±512 mV. A red clipping warning means the peaks are flattened — reduce speed for that reading.
  • The kit screen shows the largest |peak|; this page shows half the peak-to-peak value, which ignores any small DC offset. They can differ by a few mV.
  • Speed (rpm) is worked out from the Coil 1 EMF frequency, so it reads 0 when the motor is stopped or the signal is too small.
  • Phase is the phase difference of the fundamental (best-fit sine) of each coil. If the waveforms have different shapes, the peaks and zero crossings on the screen can be several degrees further apart or closer together than this number.
  • Demo mode, two coils: the Coil 2 position slider moves the simulated Coil 2 around the magnet. A coil that faces the magnet from angle α sees the flux ∝ cos(θ − α), so its EMF lags Coil 1 by α: the phase reading follows the slider degree for degree. Two coils facing each other across the magnet (180°) see opposite flux, so their EMFs are 180° apart; swapping one coil's two leads adds another 180°. That is why the real kit, with its coils on opposite sides of the rail, reads about 0°. Start at 180° with leads reversed (like the kit), then untick it or move the slider and watch the phase.
  • Alignment check: with the coils placed symmetrically, the phase should read about 0° in both Forward and Reverse. The same number with opposite sign in the two directions comes from the geometry (magnet or coil position); a number that keeps its sign would be a timing error.

Troubleshooting

  • No port in the list – tick show all serial ports; try another cable or USB socket.
  • “Failed to open serial port” – another program is using it. Close the Arduino Serial Monitor/Plotter or any other tab connected to the kit.
  • Connected but no data – the kit may be running old (offline) firmware. It needs EMF_Teaching_Kit_Dual_Coil_USB.
  • “ADS1115 not found” – check the Stemma QT cable between the Feather and the ADC board.
